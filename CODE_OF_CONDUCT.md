# ACME Foundation Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the
ACME Foundation ecosystem a harassment-free, zero-fluff, and intellectually rigorous
experience for everyone.

## Invariant Ground Rules
1. **Least-Action Focus:** Keep communication dense, factual, and strictly anchored to ground truth. Avoid cognitive churn, bureaucratic posturing, and noise.
2. **Empirical Verification:** Criticisms must cite invariant rules, failing test traces, or verifiable specifications (SPEC.md).
3. **Respect & Autonomy:** Foster psychological safety and objective peer-review across human and agentic contributors alike.

Enforcement is overseen by the ACME Foundation Core Architecture Board.
