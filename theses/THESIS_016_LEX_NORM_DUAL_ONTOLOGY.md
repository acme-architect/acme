## 1. SCOPE & INTENT
Resolves the dialectical tension between architectural rigidity and bureaucratic sclerosis. 
A purely rigid system collapses under operational friction; a purely flexible system degrades into entropic drift. 
This specification establishes a bifurcated epistemic closure:
1. **The Lex (Inviolable Invariant):** Binary constraints ($\text{DoF} = 0$) where breach results in immediate evaluation failure (`REJECT`).
2. **The Norm (Adaptive Standard):** Gradient heuristics ($\text{DoF} \ge 1$) that offer contextual best practices, patterns, and performance guidance without halting compilation.

## 3. VIA NEGATIVA & INVARIANTS
* **INVARIANT 1 (No Norm-to-Lex Inflation):** Advisory best practices, styling guidelines, or dynamic thresholds must never be elevated to `Lex` ($\text{DoF}=0$) without formal Review Board consensus.
* **INVARIANT 2 (No Lex Erosion):** A `Lex` rule can never be relaxed into an advisory `Norm` within a local workspace. Downgrading an invariant is an architectural breach.
* **INVARIANT 3 (Mandatory Bifurcation in Specifications):** Every future RFC, thesis, and canonical spec must explicitly separate its outputs into Section A (The Lex / Mandatory Bounds) and Section B (The Norms / Recommended Patterns).
