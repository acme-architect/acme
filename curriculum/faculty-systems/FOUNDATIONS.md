## MODULE SYS-101: THERMODYNAMIC EQUILIBRIUM & INVARIANT ARCHITECTURE

---

### 1. FOUNDATIONAL AXIOMS
* **Second Law of Thermodynamics (Clausius, Boltzmann):** Isolated cognitive systems naturally evolve toward maximal entropy (semantic drift, hallucination, structural decay). Sustaining order requires work and bounded energy dissipation.
* **Principle of Least Action (PLA - Hamilton, Lagrange):** The physical trajectory between two states minimizes the action integral ($\delta S = 0$). Cognitive workflows must minimize computational overhead, token bloat, and redundant state transitions.
* **Autopoiesis & Homeostasis (Ashby, Maturana, Varela):** Viable systems reproduce their internal organization through circular self-referential boundary preservation against external perturbation.

### 2. CORE TOPICS & RESEARCH VECTORS
1. **Thermal Load & Backpropagation:** Quantifying semantic saturation in multi-agent networks; dynamic cooling through zero-friction routing and pruning.
2. **Mean Squared Error (MSE) Optimization (Friston Free Energy):** Minimizing the divergence between generative internal models and empirical ground truth.
3. **Bifurcation & Dynamic Phase Transitions:** Structural adaptation of system topology under acute pressure without boundary rupture.

### 3. THESIS CANDIDATE REQUIREMENTS
* Every thesis submitted to `faculty-systems` must specify an executable invariant contract, an empirical baseline, and automated validation tests executing in $O(1)$ or $O(n)$ deterministic complexity.

## MODULE SYS-106: STEP-LEVEL PROCESS REWARD MODELS (PRM) & TREE SEARCH
> **Originating Thesis:** Node-024 (RFC-023) | **Focus:** Intermediate Pruning & Deterministic Backtracking

### 1. FOUNDATIONAL AXIOMS
* **Step-Level PRM Validation:** Evaluating cognitive workflows solely at terminal outcome (ORM) invites cascading errors. Each intermediate step must be scored individually ($S_{\text{step}} \ge 0.70$).
* **Deterministic Backtracking:** When an exploratory branch is pruned or exhausted, the search engine must cleanly unwind to the nearest sound ancestor without stochastic looping.

### 2. CORE TOPICS & RESEARCH VECTORS
1. **Tree of Thoughts Search Horizons:** Strict mathematical bounds on search depth ($D_{\max}$) and beam width ($B_{\max}$) to avert combinatorial explosion.
2. **Cryptographic Path Certification:** Merkle logging of validated reasoning paths and intermediate score certificates.
